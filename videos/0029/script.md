# 0029 — three clock hands, meetings a day: the video script

The storyboard of the video: the captions of every beat (Hebrew, a `> ` line per caption
line, a blank line between captions), in the order the video shows them. The captions are
exactly what the video shows; `videos/render.py` fails when scene.py's captions differ.

No code: each part is a proof step by step. Part א counts each pair of hands (1,416, 1,438, 22) and removes the moments counted three times (00:00 and 12:00): 2,872. The page's clock box is played pair after pair (`BOXES['pairs']`), then "next triple". Part ב: the gap grows linearly, so the meetings are every T = ti tj / (tj − ti); the three interval counts on the page's gapline box (`BOXES['gap']`: 3T and 2.75T); the triple meeting only every 43,200 s; 2,872 again.

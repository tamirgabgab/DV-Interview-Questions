# 0030 — the spider and the fly on a box: the video script

The storyboard of the video: the captions of every beat (Hebrew, a `> ` line per caption
line, a blank line between captions), in the order the video shows them. The captions are
exactly what the video shows; `videos/render.py` fails when scene.py's captions differ.

The owner's choices (2026-11-02): a 3D box with translucent faces, each its own colour (kept in the unfolding); part א the trap of 42 first, then 40 through five faces, then the table of every unfolding; part ב the idea only, no code typed: the 16 unfoldings as thumbnails with the idea in three steps, the strip, and the page's strip box at the end; about 5–6 minutes.

# 0027 — a cake shared by an arithmetic sequence: the video script

The storyboard of the video: the captions of every beat (Hebrew, a `> ` line per caption
line, a blank line between captions), in the order the video shows them. The captions are
exactly what the video shows; `videos/render.py` fails when scene.py's captions differ.

No code (the page has none). The division (the owner, 2026-11-03): a cake strip from which each person cuts a slice of what is left, and under it a bar chart of the shares built bar by bar. Part א: a = 0.01, d = 0.1 person by person (the first five slowly, the rest at once, the eleventh taking the rest), the peak at person 4 (0.2158), then the page's proof: R_k and S_k, the ratio of neighbours, the quadratic condition, T = 0.2702, the fractions against T, S_4 = 0.31 · 0.6961, and the page's box person by person (`BOXES['people']`). Part ב: f(p_k) < d with f increasing, the people as points climbing the curve against y = d (one peak), the threshold and the formula for k checked on part א, the capped last person in one line, the tie on a = d = 1/12 (persons 3 and 4, 55/288 each), the case d > 1/2 in one sentence pointing to the follow-ups, and the box's proof panel (`BOXES['proof']`: d = 0.1, then 0.25).

# 0032 — wolf, goat and cabbage: the video script

The storyboard of the video: the captions of every beat (Hebrew, a `> ` line per caption
line, a blank line between captions), in the order the video shows them. The captions are
exactly what the video shows; `videos/render.py` fails when scene.py's captions differ.

The river is drawn in Manim with simple icons (the owner, 2026-11-01): a farmer with a hat, a wolf, a goat and a cabbage, no Hebrew in the picture, the near bank on the left as in the page's box. Part א: why the goat goes first, the seven crossings, the page's box by its buttons (`BOXES['plan']`, with the two crossings it refuses: the wolf first, and going back alone after the wolf), the proof that seven is the least (at least 3 forward and 2 back; with exactly five the second item is left with the goat; so a return carries an item: 7), the 16 states and 10 safe ones, and the trap after crossing 3. Part ב: the state as four bits (C G W F = 8 4 2 1), the 16 states with the 6 unsafe ones struck, the 10 safe ones as a graph by BFS layers (0, 5, 4, {7, 13}, {2, 8}, 11, 10, 15), the page's code typed (the constants and `river_safe`, then `river_plan` in a 15-line window), and its run beside the box's proof panel (`BOXES['bfs']`), the trace jumping from the end of one layer to the end of the next (the owner, 2026-11-01); the result is the plan of part א. Then the proof, the cost and the trap of a state without the farmer.

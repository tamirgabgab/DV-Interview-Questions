## Chapter סעיף ב – קוד : כל מספר אנשים

Part ב: the state (a mask of who is across and the torch's side, 2^N · 2 = 32), BFS against Dijkstra (two plans of five edges, 19 and 17), the page's `bridge` typed group by group (the docstring line left out of the picture), then its run on 1, 2, 5, 10: the first pops line by line, then from pop to pop (the cursor jumps to each group's last line), the variables and a log of the popped states beside the code. The goal is first pushed at 19 and improved to 17 before it is popped. The walk back by the parents, the box playing the plan, the cost, and the trap of putting the time into the state.

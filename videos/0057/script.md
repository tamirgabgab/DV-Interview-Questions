# 0057 — ants on a rod: the video script

The storyboard of the video: the captions of every beat (Hebrew, a `> ` line per caption
line, a blank line between captions), in the order the video shows them. The captions are
exactly what the video shows; `videos/render.py` fails when scene.py's captions differ.

The owner's choice (2026-11-01): parts א, ב and ג in full, part ד briefly. The ants are drawn in Manim; a collision of two ants looks exactly like two ghosts passing through each other.

## Chapter סעיף ב – כמה התנגשויות

The five crossings of the ghost lines marked. A pair meets only when the left one goes right and the right one left, at (b - a)/2, before either leaves. The count per ant going left (1 + 2 + 2 = 5); at most k(n - k), 50 · 50 = 2500. `collisions` typed and traced ant by ant, the ant in hand framed under the code. The cost, and the trap of a pair facing apart.

> עכשיו נכתוב את זה ב-Python.
